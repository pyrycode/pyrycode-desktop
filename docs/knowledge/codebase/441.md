## Summary

Real-daemon twin of the merged fake-stack spec [#456](456.md): the same two `WorkspacePickerSheet`
(#383) round-trips — recent-workspaces and create-folder — driven against a real spawned claude-less
`pyry` (the [#439 realDaemon fixture](../features/real-daemon-credential-light-e2e.md),
`spawnClaude:false, seedPromoted:false`) instead of `conversationStateFake`. This is the credential-light
tier that would have caught the `promote_conversation` gap (pyrycode/pyrycode#949): a daemon that defines
a wire verb and registry op but registers no handler answers `unsupported` on the real wire, while the
fake suite stays green because a fake answers anything. Both verbs are pure registry ops daemon-side
(never touch claude), so they prove against a real daemon deterministically and cheaply.

`seedPromoted:false` (unlike #440's `true`) makes the single seed a Recent **discussion** rather than a
Channel — its never-messaged, claude-less thread renders empty, satisfying the `WorkspaceChip`'s
realizability gate (`isEmpty && conversation !== null && !conversation.is_promoted`,
`ConversationScreen.tsx:436-448`). The spec pairs, gates readiness on `.channel-list__save` (the
non-promoted seed's affordance, standing in for #440's `.channel-list__rename` pencil), opens the seeded
row into its thread, asserts "Change workspace" is enabled (pinning the post-#448 precondition), opens
the picker, asserts the recent-workspaces reply resolved the picker out of not-loaded (tolerant of rows
or the loaded-empty copy), then drives create-folder to completion and asserts the whole picker tree
unmounts.

**Ran green end-to-end**, not just skip-clean: the CI/dev machine had a compatible `pyry` on PATH, so the
spec executed the full real-wire drive in 2.4s rather than skip-gating on a missing binary. This live-verified
both architect open questions against the real stack in one run (see Patterns established).

**change-workspace remains unasserted** (the parent's rescoped three-verb framing) — the create-folder
flow still *sends* `change_workspace` over the real wire (the app's auto-chain after
`workspace_folder_created`), but the picker's close is gated on `workspace_folder_created`'s reply, not
`change_workspace`'s (`conversation_updated`, a no-op for `activeConversationStore`), so the daemon's
handling of `change_workspace` has no DOM surface on this tier. Its client→wire contract is covered by
#456; daemon-side handling by pyrycode/pyrycode#980.

## Patterns established

- **"Picker gone" must be asserted on an unconditionally-rendered element, not a state-dependent one, when the state is daemon-derived.** The fake twin #456 closed on `.workspace-picker__row` count-0 — sound there because its seeded recents were always non-empty by fixture construction. On the real tier the recent-workspaces list is derived server-side from the registry; a future daemon that excluded the current cwd from the fold would render zero rows even while the picker stays open, making a row-count-0 close check pass vacuously. `.workspace-picker__other` (the create-folder entry) renders unconditionally whenever the picker body renders, so its disappearance is the sound "picker unmounted" proof regardless of the recents outcome. Generalizes beyond this spec: on a real-daemon tier, prefer a close/unmount assertion on a DOM element whose presence doesn't depend on the same round-trip already under test.
- **Real-daemon siblings that open a *seeded* row (not a FAB-created one) need their own readiness gate keyed to the seed's promotion state**, not a generic "a row exists" check. #440's readiness gate was `.channel-list__rename` (a promoted seed); this ticket's is `.channel-list__save` (a non-promoted, discussion seed) — the section-affordance-as-readiness-signal idiom #440 established for scoping now doubles as the strongest available handshake-complete proof for a non-promoted seed too.
- **A tolerant (loaded-vs-not-loaded) assertion, not a row-count assertion, is the AC-faithful form when the reply content is daemon-derived and not pinned by the fixture.** OQ-a resolved to exactly one row on the live daemon, but the spec asserts `.workspace-picker__row, .workspace-picker__empty` (either), matching the AC's explicit wording and staying resilient to a future daemon that changes the fold (e.g. excludes the current cwd → empty list) — either outcome still proves the handler responded.

## Lessons learned

*Lifted from the PR body's own "Lessons learned" section.*

- Verified live, not just skip-clean: because a compatible `pyry` was on PATH, the spec ran GREEN
  end-to-end, confirming both architect open questions against the real stack in a single run —
  recent-workspaces resolved out of not-loaded (OQ-a: the seeded registry's one `cwd` folds to exactly
  one row) and create-folder completed → `workspace_folder_created` → the picker closed (OQ-b: the
  seed's `$HOME/work` cwd is writable, so the daemon's `mkdir` succeeds rather than replying
  `workspace_folder_rejected`). A timeout on either half would have been a genuine #949-class daemon gap
  to file, never a flake to paper over — this run gave the harness a chance to prove that stance without
  needing it.
- "Picker gone" is asserted on `.workspace-picker__other` (rendered unconditionally while the picker is
  open), not `.workspace-picker__row` — see Patterns established above.
